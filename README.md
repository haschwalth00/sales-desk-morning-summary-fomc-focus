# Sales Desk Morning Summary – FOMC Focus

## Overview

This project is a simulated **Sales & Trading morning market summary**, reflecting how real sales desks communicate key macro developments to clients and internal teams. It covers overnight moves in USD rates, global currencies, and market expectations ahead of a major **FOMC policy announcement**.

## Author

**Vanshwardhan Singh**

## Objective

To demonstrate the ability to:
- Write clearly and concisely for a financial markets audience
- Analyze macroeconomic signals, central bank policy expectations, and market positioning
- Interpret data trends (inflation, Treasury yields) and link them to trading implications

## Summary Highlights

- **USD Rates:** Stable overnight with slight upward movement in 10-year Treasury yields.
- **Inflation:** CPI readings remain elevated (~3–4%), above Fed’s 2% target.
- **FOMC Outlook:** Consensus expects no rate hike today, but continued hawkish tone and potential future tightening.
- **Market Focus:** Powell’s tone, economic projections, and narrative around “higher for longer.”

## Supporting Data

- `10-year treasury yield 22sep(1).csv`: Historical trend of U.S. Treasury yields
- `Inflation_(1).xlsx`: Recent U.S. CPI data used to support inflation commentary

## Use Case

This summary simulates the type of pre-market briefing circulated to institutional clients or internally at an investment bank sales desk. It emphasizes clarity, relevance, and data-driven market framing.
